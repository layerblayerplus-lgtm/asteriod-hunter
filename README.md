### Here all the upgrades in the game
- upgrade - use 
- solar panel - generates power
- coppertube - cools the laser turret to allow faster fire rate
- waterstorage - stores water
- wire - send electricity to the water storage
- hydrogen tank - stores hydrogen
- oxygen tank - stores oxygen
- carbon burner - stores carbon and can turn 1 carbon and 2 oxygen's into to Co2
- oxygen pipe - allows oxygen to flow to carbon burner
- Sabatier reactor - turns hydrogen and Co2 into ch4
- hydrogen pipe - allows hydrogen to flow to Sabatier reactor
- Co2 pipe - allows oxygen to flow to Sabatier reactor
- methane pipe - allows methane to flow from sabatier reactor to methane tank
- methane tank - stores methane

- ### here all asteriod types
- type - materials- rarity
- carbonaceous asteriod - water and carbon - very common
- stoney asteriod - iron and silicon - uncommon
- metalic asteriod -  (2nd most)iron and copper - kinda rare
- metal asteriod - (most)iron, (most)copper and nickel - very rare

- ### user controls
- input - output
- E - enter shop
- R - resume game
- left arrow or A key -turn turret left 
- right arrow or D key -turn turret right
- up arrow or W key -speed up laserbeems (only if you have the upgrade)
- down arrow or S key - activate mega laser beem
- mouse clicks - for pressing buttons or buying upgrades

- ### sounds
- laserbeem shot
- megalaserbeem shot
- laser beem asteriod hit
- megalaserbeem asteriod hit
- asteriod hit ground
- laser sound from carbon burner
- sabatier reactor sound
- laser charging sound from megalaserbeem
- bubbling sound from electrolysis
